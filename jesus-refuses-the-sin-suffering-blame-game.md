# Jesus Refuses the Sin–Suffering Blame Game

**Passage:** John 9:1–7; Luke 13:1–5; Job 1:1–2:10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says  
**Topic:** Suffering & sin

**BIBLE BITE | Jesus Refuses the Sin–Suffering Blame Game**  
**Passage:** John 9:1–7; Luke 13:1–5; Job 1:1–2:10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says

**The bite:** Scripture sometimes connects particular suffering with particular sin, but it rejects that as a universal diagnosis. Jesus denies that the man’s blindness resulted from his or his parents’ sin and rejects the idea that disaster victims were worse sinners.[1] Job is introduced as blameless even before calamity strikes.[2]

**Why it matters:** Christians may locate suffering within a fallen creation, as Howerton does, without presuming that a sufferer caused it; Jesus redirects blame toward mercy and God’s work.[3]
