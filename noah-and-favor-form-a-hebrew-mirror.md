# Noah and Favor Form a Hebrew Mirror

**Passage:** Genesis 5:29; 6:8–9 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Plausible  
**Topic:** Hebrew wordplay & grace

**BIBLE BITE | Noah and Favor Form a Hebrew Mirror**  
**Passage:** Genesis 5:29; 6:8–9 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Plausible

**The bite:** Noah’s Hebrew name, *Noaḥ* (n-ḥ), is associated with “rest,” while the word translated “favor,” *ḥen* (ḥ-n), reverses those two consonants.[1] Scholars have proposed intentional wordplay: amid universal corruption, “Noah found favor.”[2] Yet *Noaḥ* and *ḥen* are different words, not one word spelled backward.

**Why it matters:** The possible sound-play sharpens Genesis’s dramatic reversal—judgment surrounds Noah, but God’s favorable regard opens a path of preservation.
