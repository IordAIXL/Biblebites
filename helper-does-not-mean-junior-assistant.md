# “Helper” Does Not Mean Junior Assistant

**Passage:** Genesis 2:18–23 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Marriage & Hebrew

**BIBLE BITE | “Helper” Does Not Mean Junior Assistant**  
**Passage:** Genesis 2:18–23 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** God calls the woman an *ʿezer kenegdo*: a “helper corresponding to him.” *ʿEzer* does not imply a subordinate assistant—Scripture frequently uses the noun for God as Israel’s help—while *kenegdo* conveys someone matching or corresponding to the man.[1] The animals do not supply this counterpart; the woman shares his very bone and flesh.[2]

**Why it matters:** Howerton’s “face-to-face” marriage is a fitting application of correspondence, but the Hebrew itself establishes a suitable counterpart, not a three-position marriage model.
