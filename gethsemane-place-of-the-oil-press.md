# Gethsemane: The Place of the Oil Press

**Passage:** Matthew 26:36–46 (NIV)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Language & culture

**BIBLE BITE | Gethsemane: The Place of the Oil Press**  
**Passage:** Matthew 26:36–46 (NIV)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** *Gethsemane* probably comes from Aramaic for “oil press,” fitting its location at the Mount of Olives.[1] Ancient processors first crushed olives, then pressed the pulp under a weighted beam to release the oil.[2] Matthew places Jesus there “sorrowful and troubled,” submitting his will to the Father before his arrest.[3]

**Why it matters:** The setting makes “Jesus being pressed” a vivid theological picture—but an interpretation, not symbolism Matthew explicitly explains.

**Caution:** The place-name and olive-processing background are well supported. The Gospel does not say that local people read Jesus’ anguish as an enacted olive-press symbol, so that stronger claim should not be presented as historical fact.
