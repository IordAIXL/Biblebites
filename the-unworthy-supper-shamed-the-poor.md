# The “Unworthy” Supper Shamed the Poor

**Passage:** 1 Corinthians 11:17–34 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Communion & justice

**BIBLE BITE | The “Unworthy” Supper Shamed the Poor**  
**Passage:** 1 Corinthians 11:17–34 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Corinthian communion took place within a real fellowship meal: some diners became drunk while others went hungry.[1] Wealthier believers could arrive earlier and bring more food, turning the Lord’s table into another display of social rank.[2] In context, eating “unworthily” describes a divided manner of eating—not a requirement to become personally worthy first.

**Why it matters:** “Discerning the body” includes recognizing the church body beside you; remembrance of Christ cannot coexist with humiliating poorer believers.
