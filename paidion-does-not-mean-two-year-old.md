# *Paidion* Does Not Mean “Two-Year-Old”

**Passage:** Matthew 2:7–16; Luke 2:12–17 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Christmas & Magi

**BIBLE BITE | *Paidion* Does Not Mean “Two-Year-Old”**  
**Passage:** Matthew 2:7–16; Luke 2:12–17 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Matthew calls Jesus a *paidion* when the Magi visit, but Luke uses the same word for Jesus on the night the shepherds find the newborn.[1] The Greek word can mean an infant or a child; it does not mean “grown-up boy.”[2] Herod’s order covering boys two and under supplies an upper limit, not Jesus’ exact age.[3]

**Why it matters:** The Magi may have arrived sometime after the birth, but neither *paidion* nor Herod’s range proves that Jesus was two years old.
