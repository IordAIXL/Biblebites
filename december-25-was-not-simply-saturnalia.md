# December 25 Was Not Simply Saturnalia

**Passage:** Luke 2:1–20 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed  
**Topic:** Christmas history

**BIBLE BITE | December 25 Was Not Simply Saturnalia**  
**Passage:** Luke 2:1–20 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed

**The bite:** Luke gives no date—or even season—for Jesus’ birth.[1] Saturnalia normally ran December 17–23, not December 25.[2] Scholars debate whether Christmas’s date reflects nearby Roman solar festivals, an early Christian calculation placing Jesus’ conception on March 25 and birth nine months later, or interacting influences.[3]

**Why it matters:** December 25 is a later liturgical date, but saying Christians simply recycled Saturnalia turns a debated history into a settled story.
