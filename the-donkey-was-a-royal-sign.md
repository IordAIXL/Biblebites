# The Donkey Was a Royal Sign

**Passage:** Matthew 21:1–11; Zechariah 9:9–10; 1 Kings 1:32–40 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Kingship & Palm Sunday

**BIBLE BITE | The Donkey Was a Royal Sign**  
**Passage:** Matthew 21:1–11; Zechariah 9:9–10; 1 Kings 1:32–40 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Jesus’ donkey was humble, but it was not merely a poor person’s ride. Solomon rode David’s mule to his coronation, and Zechariah’s promised king approaches on a donkey while banishing chariots, warhorses, and battle bows.[1] Jesus was staging a royal claim—peaceful, prophetic, and unmistakably Davidic.[2]

**Why it matters:** Palm Sunday reveals not a king without power, but a king defining his power through humility and peace rather than military force.
