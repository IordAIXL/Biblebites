# Samaritans Were Not Simply “Babylonian Half-Breeds”

**Passage:** 2 Kings 17:5–41; John 4:7–26  
**Evidence:** Disputed  
**Topic:** Samaritans & historical context

**BIBLE BITE | Samaritans Were Not Simply “Babylonian Half-Breeds”**  
**Passage:** 2 Kings 17:5–41; John 4:7–26  
**Evidence:** Disputed

**The bite:** Second Kings attributes Samaria’s fall and resettlement to **Assyria**, not Babylon, and describes imported peoples worshiping YHWH alongside other gods.[1] Later Judean tradition used this account to question Samaritan ancestry; Samaritans claimed descent from Israel’s northern tribes. Modern specialists regard New Testament Samaritan origins as more complex than one intermarriage story.[2]

**Why it matters:** Jesus’ Samaritan encounters cross a centuries-old dispute over ancestry, Scripture, and the proper sanctuary—not merely ethnic dislike of supposed “half-breeds.”
