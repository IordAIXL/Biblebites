# Pentecost’s Tongues Were Understood

**Passage:** Acts 2:1–13; 1 Corinthians 14:1–28 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says  
**Topic:** Holy Spirit & tongues

**BIBLE BITE | Pentecost’s Tongues Were Understood**  
**Passage:** Acts 2:1–13; 1 Corinthians 14:1–28 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says

**The bite:** At Pentecost, pilgrims hear God’s deeds proclaimed in their own native languages; the miracle communicates across difference rather than erasing it.[1] In Corinth, however, Paul describes speech that others cannot understand without interpretation.[2] The two scenes should not be blended as though Scripture describes every experience of “tongues” identically.[3]

**Why it matters:** Whatever one’s view of spiritual gifts today, the New Testament repeatedly makes intelligibility and the building up of others central to public worship.
