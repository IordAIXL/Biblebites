# Mary Sings in Hannah’s Key

**Passage:** Luke 1:46–55; 1 Samuel 2:1–10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Mary & biblical echoes

**BIBLE BITE | Mary Sings in Hannah’s Key**  
**Passage:** Luke 1:46–55; 1 Samuel 2:1–10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Mary’s Magnificat is patterned on Hannah’s ancient prayer.[1] Both women praise the God who overturns human status: the proud and powerful fall, while the lowly and hungry are raised and filled.[2] Luke thus introduces Mary not as a silent nativity figure but as a Scripture-shaped prophetic voice announcing the social shape of God’s kingdom.

**Why it matters:** The reversals Jesus later proclaims in Luke are already being sung before his birth.
