# Dinosaurs Existed—But Genesis Does Not Date Them

**Passage:** Genesis 1:20–25; Job 40:15–24 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Bible & science

**BIBLE BITE | Dinosaurs Existed—But Genesis Does Not Date Them**  
**Passage:** Genesis 1:20–25; Job 40:15–24 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Fossils strongly establish dinosaurs: non-avian species disappeared about 66 million years ago, while birds are living dinosaurs.[1] *Homo sapiens* appears roughly 300,000 years ago in the fossil record.[2] Genesis names broad creature groups, not modern taxa or dates; Job calls its creature *Behemoth*, not “dinosaur.”[3]

**Why it matters:** Dinosaurs need not threaten faith, but claims that humans lived with non-avian dinosaurs—or carried them on the ark—come from a particular chronology, not an explicit biblical identification.

**Caution:** Young-earth creationists reject the mainstream dates and sometimes identify Behemoth as a dinosaur. Job’s poetic description does not settle that identification.
