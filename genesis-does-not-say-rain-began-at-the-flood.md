# Genesis Does Not Say Rain Began at the Flood

**Passage:** Genesis 2:4–7; 7:4, 11–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says  
**Topic:** Creation & flood

**BIBLE BITE | Genesis Does Not Say Rain Began at the Flood**  
**Passage:** Genesis 2:4–7; 7:4, 11–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Text says

**The bite:** Genesis 2:5 says God had not yet sent rain in the scene before humanity and cultivation; verse 6 says a stream watered the ground.[1] Genesis 7 later describes forty days of rain, but never calls it earth’s first rainfall.[2]

**Why it matters:** “No rain until Noah” stretches a statement about conditions in Genesis 2 into a claim about the entire pre-Flood climate.

**Caution:** Some interpreters infer a rainless pre-Flood world, but that conclusion is not explicit in the text.
