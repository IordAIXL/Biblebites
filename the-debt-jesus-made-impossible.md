# The Debt Jesus Made Impossible

**Passage:** Matthew 18:21–35 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Money & mercy

**BIBLE BITE | The Debt Jesus Made Impossible**  
**Passage:** Matthew 18:21–35 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** One talent equaled about 6,000 denarii, so the first servant’s 10,000-talent debt was 60 million days’ wages—600,000 times the second servant’s 100-denarius debt.[1] Jesus chose a liability “beyond conception,” not a realistic personal loan.[2]

**Why it matters:** The smaller debt was still serious—roughly one hundred workdays—but the contrast makes forgiven grace incalculably larger than the wrongs disciples are asked to forgive.
