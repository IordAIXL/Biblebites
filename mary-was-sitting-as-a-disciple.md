# Mary Was Sitting as a Disciple

**Passage:** Luke 10:38–42 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported  
**Topic:** Women & discipleship

**BIBLE BITE | Mary Was Sitting as a Disciple**  
**Passage:** Luke 10:38–42 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Strongly supported

**The bite:** Mary’s place “at the Lord’s feet” is more than a picture of quiet devotion: it portrays the posture of a pupil receiving a teacher’s instruction.[1] In a culture where sustained religious study was usually associated with men, Mary occupies a disciple’s place—and Jesus defends her choice.[2]

**Why it matters:** The contrast is not “work bad, contemplation good.” Martha is distracted by service; Mary gives Jesus’ word the undivided attention expected of a disciple.
