# The Vapor Canopy Is a Model, Not the Text

**Passage:** Genesis 1:6–8; 7:11–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed  
**Topic:** Creation & flood

**BIBLE BITE | The Vapor Canopy Is a Model, Not the Text**  
**Passage:** Genesis 1:6–8; 7:11–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed

**The bite:** Genesis describes “waters above” the sky and later the “windows of the heavens,” but never identifies a planet-wrapping vapor shield or says it prevented rain.[1] The canopy is a modern scientific reconstruction; even Answers in Genesis now rejects a substantial canopy because the text does not clearly teach it and the model creates lethal heat problems.[2]

**Why it matters:** A proposed mechanism for the Flood should not be presented as something Genesis itself explains.

**Caution:** Some young-earth creationists still defend versions of the canopy. The model is disputed within creationist circles and rejected by mainstream atmospheric science.
