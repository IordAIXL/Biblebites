# Was Zacchaeus the Short One?

**Passage:** Luke 19:1–10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed  
**Topic:** Greek ambiguity & Zacchaeus

**BIBLE BITE | Was Zacchaeus the Short One?**  
**Passage:** Luke 19:1–10 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed

**The bite:** Translations normally say Zacchaeus could not see because **he** was short. The Greek pronoun is grammatically ambiguous, however: “he” could refer to Zacchaeus or to Jesus.[1] Nearly all scholars and translations choose Zacchaeus, but Isaac Soon has recently argued that Luke may be describing a short Jesus.[2]

**Why it matters:** The story’s certain center is not either man’s height: a despised tax collector seeks to see Jesus, and Jesus sees, calls, and welcomes him before the crowd does.
