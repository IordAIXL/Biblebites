# Satan Asked to Sift All the Disciples

**Passage:** Luke 22:31–34 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Text says  
**Topic:** Spiritual warfare & language

**BIBLE BITE | Satan Asked to Sift All the Disciples**  
**Passage:** Luke 22:31–34 (NET)  
**Evidence:** Text says

**The bite:** English “you” hides Luke’s shift in number. Satan demands to sift **you all**—the Greek pronoun is plural, encompassing the disciples—but Jesus says, “I have prayed for **you**, Simon,” using the singular.[1] Jesus then tells the restored Peter to strengthen his brothers.

**Why it matters:** The attack targets the community; Jesus’ intercession restores a failing disciple so he can steady the others. Peter’s denial is neither minimized nor allowed to become his final identity.
