# Daniel and the Magi: An Unproven Link

**Passage:** Daniel 2:48; Matthew 2:1–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Interpretation  
**Topic:** Christmas & Magi

**BIBLE BITE | Daniel and the Magi: An Unproven Link**  
**Passage:** Daniel 2:48; Matthew 2:1–12 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Interpretation

**The bite:** Daniel was appointed over Babylon’s wise men, while Matthew’s *magi* came “from the East.”[1] But neither book says Daniel taught messianic prophecies to their order, nor that the Bethlehem visitors were their descendants. *Magi* could designate Iranian priestly specialists or, more broadly, astrologers and diviners.[2]

**Why it matters:** A memorable story about faith passed down for six centuries may preach well, but it should not be introduced as a Bible fact.
