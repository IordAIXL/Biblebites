# Shepherds Do Not Date Christmas

**Passage:** Luke 2:8–20 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Plausible  
**Topic:** Christmas history

**BIBLE BITE | Shepherds Do Not Date Christmas**  
**Passage:** Luke 2:8–20 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Plausible

**The bite:** Luke says shepherds were living outdoors and guarding their flock at night, but supplies no month.[1] The detail may fit a warmer season or spring lambing, yet specialists warn it cannot establish a precise birth season.[2] Even readings that use the shepherds as a clue commonly suggest spring—not “almost certainly summer.”[3]

**Why it matters:** Luke’s focus is who first hears the good news, not giving readers a hidden calendar date.
