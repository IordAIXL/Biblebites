**Topic:** Biblical echoes & obedience

**BIBLE BITE | Paul’s Shipwreck May Be a Reverse Jonah**  
**Passage:** Jonah 1:1–16; Acts 27:13–44 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Plausible

**The bite:** Both accounts send a prophet toward a Gentile capital, then portray a violent sea, terrified sailors, and cargo thrown overboard.[1] The contrast is sharper: disobedient Jonah endangers his shipmates, while obedient Paul becomes God’s messenger of hope and all 276 aboard survive. A recent literary study argues that Acts deliberately presents Paul as a “true prophet” over against Jonah.[2]

**Why it matters:** The comparison makes obedience life-giving—but Acts never names Jonah, so this should be taught as a plausible literary echo rather than a proven authorial signal.
