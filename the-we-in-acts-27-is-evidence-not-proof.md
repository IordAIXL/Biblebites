# The “We” in Acts 27 Is Evidence, Not Proof

**Passage:** Acts 27:1–28:16 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed  
**Topic:** Acts & eyewitness history

**BIBLE BITE | The “We” in Acts 27 Is Evidence, Not Proof**  
**Passage:** Acts 27:1–28:16 (NRSVue)  
**Evidence:** Disputed

**The bite:** Acts suddenly narrates Paul’s voyage as “we,” while supplying unusually precise routes, soundings, and sailing actions.[1] This naturally suggests that the narrator—or a source he preserved—was aboard, and the account’s nautical language fits ancient seafaring.[2] But Acts never names its author, and scholars still debate whether “we” proves the author’s personal eyewitness participation.[3]

**Why it matters:** The voyage bears strong marks of proximity to real travel, while historical confidence should remain more careful than “the pronoun settles authorship.”
